## The $1.3T Question: Will Insurance Collapse?

**No. But it will transform.**

Here's why:

### Reason #1: Regulation

Insurance is heavily regulated. AI can't just replace humans overnight.

**Requirements**:
- Human oversight for large claims
- Explainable AI decisions
- Fair lending compliance
- State-by-state approval

**Timeline**: 5-10 years for full automation.

### Reason #2: Complex Cases

AI handles simple claims well. Complex cases still need humans.

**Simple** (80% of claims):
- Fender bender
- Broken window
- Water damage
- Theft

**Complex** (20% of claims):
- Multi-car accidents
- Disputed liability
- Large commercial claims
- Fraud investigations

**Result**: Hybrid model. AI for simple, humans for complex.

### Reason #3: Customer Trust

People don't trust AI with their life savings. Yet.

**Current adoption**:
- 60% comfortable with AI for simple claims
- 30% comfortable with AI for underwriting
- 15% comfortable with AI for complex claims

**Trend**: Trust increasing 10-15% per year.

**Timeline**: 3-5 years for majority adoption.
