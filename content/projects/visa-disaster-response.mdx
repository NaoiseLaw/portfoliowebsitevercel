## Quick Facts

- **Role**: Product Lead & PM
- **Team**: 5 teammates
- **Challenge**: Visa x Roland Berger – commercial GenAI application
- **Methodology**: Soft Ecosystem Methodology (SEM)
- **Outcome**: Finalist; Dragons' Den presentation with feasibility feedback

## Overview

Vis(AI)D is a GenAI business solution to help SMEs recover from natural disasters. It monitors ERP activity and recommends best‑practice next steps in real time, even when resources are constrained. Built with an ecosystem mindset, the solution aligns with Visa’s focus on adaptability and innovation.

## Problem

SMEs face significant risk after natural disasters: fragmented information, disrupted operations, and limited resources. Traditional recovery playbooks are static and siloed, lacking adaptive, real‑time guidance.

## Solution

Using SEM’s adaptive, ecosystemic approach (beyond SSM), Vis(AI)D forms resilient, connected “holons” across functions. The system ingests ERP signals (e.g., inventory, payables/receivables) and generates context‑aware recommendations to prioritize actions that reduce bankruptcy risk and accelerate recovery.

## My Role

I led product framing, structured SEM artifacts, and coordinated design/engineering. I focused on translating the methodology into tangible flows and actionable recommendations, keeping the solution feasible and aligned to Visa’s dynamic capabilities.

## Impact

- Selected as Sprint Week finalist; Dragons’ Den panel provided feasibility guidance.
- Clear value hypothesis: reduce SME risk and time to operational stability.
- Opened exploration toward Visa‑adjacent support offerings for SMEs.

## Methodology: SEM in practice

SEM added a refreshing ecosystem perspective beyond SSM. We modeled holons to adapt across volatile conditions (VUCA) and anchored design choices to ecosystem constraints—resulting in a solution that is both adaptable and grounded.
