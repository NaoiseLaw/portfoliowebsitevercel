## Overview

Vis(AI)D is an innovative GenAI-powered disaster recovery platform designed to help Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) navigate the critical first 72 hours after natural disasters. Developed during LSE Sprint Week in partnership with Visa and Roland Berger, this solution combines real-time ERP monitoring with AI-driven recommendations to dramatically improve SME survival rates post-disaster.

The project addresses a critical gap: while large corporations have dedicated disaster recovery teams, SMEs—which represent 99.9% of businesses in the EU—often lack the resources and expertise to respond effectively to natural disasters, leading to a 40% failure rate within two years of a major incident.

## Challenge Context

### The Competition
- **Event**: LSE Sprint Week 2025 - Visa x Roland Berger Challenge
- **Brief**: Develop a commercial GenAI application that demonstrates Visa's innovation leadership
- **Timeline**: 5 days from concept to Dragons' Den presentation
- **Evaluation Criteria**: Commercial viability, technical feasibility, innovation, and alignment with Visa's strategic goals

### Team Composition
- **My Role**: Product Lead & Product Manager
- **Team Size**: 6 members (Product, Engineering, Design, Business Strategy)
- **Methodology**: Soft Ecosystem Methodology (SEM) for systems thinking
- **Outcome**: Selected as one of 3 finalists from 40+ competing teams

## Problem Analysis

### The SME Disaster Recovery Challenge
- **Scale**: 25 million SMEs in Europe, 99.9% of all businesses
- **Vulnerability**: 40% of SMEs fail within 2 years of a major disaster
- **Resource Constraints**: Limited capital, no dedicated disaster recovery teams
- **Information Fragmentation**: Critical data scattered across multiple systems and stakeholders
- **Time Pressure**: First 72 hours are critical for recovery trajectory

### Current Solution Gaps
- **Static Playbooks**: One-size-fits-all approaches that don't adapt to specific business contexts
- **Siloed Systems**: ERP, banking, insurance, and supply chain data exist in isolation
- **Reactive Approach**: Businesses wait for problems to manifest before taking action
- **Limited Expertise**: SMEs lack disaster recovery specialists and best-practice knowledge

## Solution Architecture

### Core Concept: Adaptive Ecosystem Intelligence
Vis(AI)D transforms disaster recovery from reactive crisis management to proactive, AI-guided resilience building through:

1. **Real-time ERP Monitoring**: Continuous analysis of business health indicators
2. **Predictive Risk Assessment**: AI models that anticipate cascading failures
3. **Contextual Recommendations**: Personalized action plans based on business type, size, and disaster impact
4. **Ecosystem Orchestration**: Coordinated response across suppliers, customers, and financial partners

### Technical Implementation

#### Data Integration Layer
- **ERP Connectors**: APIs for SAP, QuickBooks, Xero, and 15+ major SME platforms
- **Financial Data**: Real-time bank transaction monitoring via Visa's payment infrastructure
- **External Signals**: Weather data, supply chain disruption alerts, regional economic indicators
- **IoT Integration**: Sensor data from physical locations (flooding, power, connectivity)

#### AI Engine
- **Disaster Impact Modeling**: Machine learning models trained on 10,000+ historical disaster scenarios
- **Business Health Scoring**: Proprietary algorithm combining cash flow, inventory levels, and operational capacity
- **Recommendation Engine**: GPT-4 based system fine-tuned on disaster recovery best practices
- **Scenario Planning**: Monte Carlo simulations for different recovery pathways

#### User Experience
- **Crisis Dashboard**: Mobile-first interface optimized for high-stress decision making
- **Action Prioritization**: Color-coded recommendations with impact/effort scoring
- **Progress Tracking**: Real-time monitoring of recovery milestones and KPIs
- **Stakeholder Communication**: Automated updates to employees, customers, and partners

### Methodology: Soft Ecosystem Methodology (SEM)

#### Why SEM Over Traditional Approaches
Traditional Soft Systems Methodology (SSM) focuses on problem situations within organizational boundaries. SEM extends this to consider interconnected ecosystems, making it ideal for disaster scenarios where business recovery depends on external relationships and environmental factors.

#### SEM Application in Vis(AI)D
1. **Holon Identification**: Mapped SMEs as adaptive units within larger economic ecosystems
2. **Relationship Modeling**: Analyzed interdependencies between businesses, suppliers, customers, and financial institutions
3. **Adaptive Capacity**: Designed system to evolve recommendations based on changing ecosystem conditions
4. **Emergent Properties**: Leveraged network effects where collective recovery efforts strengthen individual business resilience

## Technical Challenges & Solutions

### 1. Real-time Data Integration at Scale
**Challenge**: Ingesting and processing data from 50+ different ERP systems with varying APIs and data formats
**Solution**: Built universal data adapter using GraphQL federation with automatic schema discovery

### 2. AI Model Accuracy Under Stress
**Challenge**: Ensuring AI recommendations remain accurate during chaotic disaster scenarios
**Solution**: Implemented ensemble models with confidence scoring and human-in-the-loop validation for critical decisions

### 3. Privacy and Security in Crisis
**Challenge**: Accessing sensitive business data while maintaining security during emergency situations
**Solution**: Developed zero-trust architecture with emergency access protocols and audit trails

### 4. Scalability During Disaster Events
**Challenge**: System must handle 10x normal load when disasters affect multiple regions simultaneously
**Solution**: Designed auto-scaling cloud infrastructure with disaster-specific load balancing

## Innovation Highlights

### 1. Predictive Disaster Recovery
Unlike reactive solutions, Vis(AI)D predicts potential business failures before they occur, allowing proactive intervention.

### 2. Ecosystem-Aware Recommendations
Considers not just individual business health, but the entire supply chain and customer ecosystem when generating recommendations.

### 3. Visa Integration Opportunities
- **Payment Flow Analysis**: Leverage Visa's transaction data to assess real-time business health
- **Emergency Funding**: Integration with Visa's lending products for disaster recovery financing
- **Supply Chain Visibility**: Use payment patterns to map and monitor supply chain disruptions

### 4. Adaptive Learning System
AI models continuously learn from each disaster event, improving recommendations for future incidents.
